Featured Destinations

A few places that left a mark.

South America

Bogotá, Colombia

A city of vivid street art, rich coffee culture, and a historic old town known as La Candelaria that blends colonial charm with modern energy. At 2,600 metres above sea level, the cool mountain air and buzzing creative scene make it one of South America's most exciting capitals.

Europe

Lisbon, Portugal

Perched on seven sun-drenched hills above the Tagus river, Lisbon enchants visitors with its golden-tiled buildings, soulful fado music, and some of Europe's best seafood. It is a city where ancient Moorish castles and cutting-edge design studios coexist on the same cobblestone street.

North America

San Francisco, CA

From the iconic rust-red span of the Golden Gate Bridge to the fog-draped Victorian neighbourhoods, San Francisco packs world-class food, art, and innovation into a compact, walkable city by the bay. Every hill offers a new view and every neighbourhood its own distinct personality.
